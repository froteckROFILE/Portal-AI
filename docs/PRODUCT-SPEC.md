# PORTAL AI — product rules

- Exactly ten seconds per portal.
- 9:16 vertical playback, 480p to 720p source quality.
- Permanent, readable AI disclosure.
- People are optional and selected randomly; fictional adults only.
- No real-person likeness, minors, nudity, graphic violence, readable plates,
  private addresses, logos or false news events.
- Country is thematic, not a claim that the footage is live or authentic.
- Free tier: five portals per day.
- Paid tier: unlimited catalogue travel.
- Credits: custom place/era/weather generation.
- Every generated asset passes automated and human moderation before release.
- Do not charge until availability is confirmed; refund failed generations.
